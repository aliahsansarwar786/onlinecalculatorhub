How to Use the Zakat Calculator

  1. Calculate Zakatable Assets: Aggregate cash, bank balances, liquid investments, gold/silver valuation, and trade stock, subtracting immediate debts.
  2. Enter Nisab: Look up today's market value for either 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver in your local currency and enter it.
  3. Calculate: If your wealth meets or exceeds the Nisab, the tool computes your exact 2.5% obligation.

Mathematical Formula for Zakat

The calculation follows this standard Islamic legal condition:

If Net Zakatable Wealth ≥ Nisab: Zakat = Net Zakatable Wealth × 0.025 (2.5% or 1/40th) Else: Zakat = 0 (Wealth is below Nisab exemption threshold)

Step 2: 1,000,000 × 0.025 = 25,000 PKR

Final Zakat Payable: PKR 25,000

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does this calculator ask me to enter the Nisab amount rather than fixing a price?

Gold and silver bullion market spot prices fluctuate continuously worldwide, and different Islamic jurisdictions utilize either the silver standard (612.36g) or the gold standard (87.48g). Allowing you to enter today's local Nisab ensures 100% theological precision without reliance on stale hardcoded estimates.

What happens if my wealth is below the Nisab threshold?

If your total net zakatable assets fall below the current Nisab threshold, Zakat is not due (Zakat = 0). The Nisab represents the minimum financial exemption baseline.

What assets qualify as zakatable wealth?

Zakatable assets typically include cash on hand, bank balances, gold and silver jewelry or bullion, liquid trade inventory, stocks, and mutual funds, minus short-term debts due immediately.

What percentage is payable for annual Zakat?

Standard Zakat on wealth and currency is exactly 2.5% (one fortieth or 1/40th) of net qualifying wealth that has remained above the Nisab for one complete lunar year (hawl).
