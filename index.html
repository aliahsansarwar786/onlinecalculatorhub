How to Use the Standard Calculator

  1. Enter Numbers: Click the numeric buttons (0 through 9) or type directly on your physical keyboard.
  2. Select Operations: Choose an arithmetic operator: addition (+), subtraction (−), multiplication (×), or division (÷).
  3. Use Parentheses: Use "(" and ")" to group sub-expressions and control operator precedence.
  4. Compute Result: Press the "=" button or hit Enter on your keyboard to evaluate the calculation.
  5. Memory Operations: Use M+ and M- to accumulate values, MR to recall the stored number, and MC to reset memory to zero.

Mathematical Formula & Order of Operations

Calculations adhere strictly to standard arithmetic order of operations, widely known as BODMAS / PEMDAS:

B / P : Brackets / Parentheses (evaluated first from inside out) O / E : Orders / Exponents D / M : Division and Multiplication (evaluated left to right) A / S : Addition and Subtraction (evaluated left to right)

Division by zero is undefined. In such instances, the calculator halts and outputs Error.

Worked Example

Problem: Evaluate the expression (15 + 25) × 2 − 10 ÷ 2

Step 1 (Parentheses): 15 + 25 = 40

Step 2 (Multiplication & Division): 40 × 2 = 80 and 10 ÷ 2 = 5

Step 3 (Subtraction): 80 − 5 = 75

Final Result: 75

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the standard calculator handle division by zero?

Dividing any number by zero is mathematically undefined. The calculator instantly displays an 'Error' message and allows you to clear the display or start a new calculation.

Can I use my physical computer keyboard with this calculator?

Yes, full keyboard support is active. You can type digits 0-9, arithmetic operators (+, -, *, /), decimal point (.), parentheses, Enter or = to calculate, Backspace to delete, and Escape to clear all.

How do the memory buttons (MC, MR, M+, M-) work?

M+ adds the current number to stored memory, M- subtracts it, MR recalls the stored value into your calculation, and MC resets the stored memory to zero.

Where is my calculation history stored?

History is saved solely in your local browser storage. No calculation entries or user data are ever uploaded to any web server or database.
