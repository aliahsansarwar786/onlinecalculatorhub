How to Use the Salary Calculator

  1. Choose Input Frequency: Select whether you are starting from an hourly wage, weekly pay, monthly salary, or yearly compensation.
  2. Enter Pay Amount: Type your pay rate in your chosen currency.
  3. Set Working Hours: Enter typical hours worked per week (default is standard full-time 40 hours).
  4. Review All Pay Intervals: View synchronized breakdowns for hourly, weekly, monthly, and yearly gross earnings.

Mathematical Formulas for Salary Conversion

Computations use standard 52-week annual conversion formulas:

Weekly Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours Per Week Annual Salary = Weekly Salary × 52 Weeks Monthly Salary = Annual Salary / 12 Months = (Weekly Salary × 52) / 12 Hourly Rate = Annual Salary / (52 × Hours Per Week)

Worked Example

Parameters: $20.00 per hour with 40 hours worked per week.

Step 1 (Weekly): $20.00 × 40 hours = $800.00 per week

Step 2 (Annual): $800.00 × 52 weeks = $41,600.00 per year

Step 3 (Monthly): $41,600.00 / 12 months ≈ $3,466.67 per month

Result: $800/week and $41,600/year (gross, before tax).

Frequently Asked Questions

Are tax deductions included in this salary calculator?

No. All figures produced by this tool represent gross earnings before income tax, pension contributions, social security, and local withholdings are deducted.

Why is monthly salary calculated as (Weekly * 52) / 12 instead of Weekly * 4?

A standard calendar year contains 52 weeks (365 days), meaning an average month comprises roughly 4.33 weeks rather than exactly 4 weeks. Multiplying weekly pay by 52 and dividing by 12 yields an accurate monthly figure.

Can I convert an annual salary back to an hourly rate?

Yes. Select 'Annual Salary' mode to input your yearly earnings, and the calculator will automatically divide by your weekly hours and 52 weeks to show your hourly rate.

How are unworked holidays and paid time off (PTO) handled?

This calculation assumes a 52-week paid working year (including standard paid vacation and holidays). For unpaid leave, adjust your total weekly hours or annual weeks accordingly.
