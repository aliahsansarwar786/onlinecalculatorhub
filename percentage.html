Mathematical Formulas for Percentages

The standard mathematical formulas utilized across all three modes are:

Mode 1 (?% of X) : Result = (p / 100) × X Mode 2 (X is what % of Y) : Percentage = (X / Y) × 100% (where Y ≠ 0) Mode 3 (Percentage Change): Increase : New Value = X × (1 + p / 100) = X + (X × p / 100) Decrease : New Value = X × (1 - p / 100) = X - (X × p / 100)

Worked Examples

Example A (Percentage of a Number): Find 20% of 60.

(20 / 100) × 60 = 0.20 × 60 = 12

Example B (Proportional Percentage): 12 is what percent of 60?

(12 / 60) × 100 = 0.20 × 100 = 20%

Example C (Percentage Increase): 60 increased by 20%.

Change = 60 × 0.20 = 12 ⇒ Final Value = 60 + 12 = 72

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate what percentage 12 is of 60?

Divide 12 by 60 to obtain 0.2, then multiply by 100 to get 20%. The formula is (part / whole) * 100%.

How do you calculate a 20% increase on 60?

First calculate 20% of 60, which is 12. Then add 12 to 60 to arrive at the final increased total of 72. Alternatively, multiply 60 by 1.20.

Can percentage increase or decrease exceed 100%?

Yes, an increase can easily exceed 100% when a quantity more than doubles. A decrease is typically capped at 100% when reducing a positive value to zero.

Does this tool support decimal and negative percentages?

Yes, you can input decimal percentages (such as 7.5% or 0.25%) as well as negative values for custom mathematical models.
