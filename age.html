How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Select Birth Date: Pick your day, month, and year of birth using the calendar picker.
  2. Select Reference Date: The "Age As Of" field automatically populates with today's live date, but can be altered for milestone calculations.
  3. Read Your Age: View your exact age divided into years, calendar months, and remaining days, along with cumulative days lived.

Worked Example

Birth Date: January 1, 2000

Reference Date: October 29, 2026

Step 1 (Years): 2026 − 2000 = 26 full years.

Step 2 (Months): October is month 10, January is month 1 ⇒ 10 − 1 = 9 full months.

Step 3 (Days): 29 − 1 = 28 days.

Result: 26 Years, 9 Months, 28 Days (Total of 9,798 days lived).

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator account for leap years and different month lengths?

The algorithm inspects the exact calendar days of each preceding month and accounts for leap years containing 366 days, ensuring that February 29th and irregular month lengths are accurately evaluated.

Can I calculate my age at a specific past or future date?

Yes. While it defaults to today's current date, you can customize the 'Age As Of' field to evaluate your age on any past or future historical milestone.

How is the upcoming birthday countdown determined?

The tool checks whether your birth month and day have passed in the current calendar year. If passed, it targets the following year's date and computes the remaining days and weekday.

Is my birthdate data saved or shared?

No. The calculation runs 100% inside your browser via local JavaScript. No personal dates are transmitted across the network or logged.
