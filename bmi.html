Mathematical Formulas for BMI

Body Mass Index is calculated using the following standard medical formulas:

Metric System : BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]² Imperial System : BMI = [weight (lb) / (height (in))²] × 703

Frequently Asked Questions

What is considered a healthy BMI score?

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is categorized as normal or healthy weight for adults.

Does BMI differentiate between muscle mass and body fat?

No, standard BMI calculates overall mass relative to height and does not distinguish between muscle tissue, bone density, and excess adipose body fat. Athletes may register higher BMI values despite low body fat.

What is the formula for calculating BMI in imperial units?

In imperial units, BMI is calculated as: BMI = (weight in pounds / (height in inches squared)) * 703.

Is this BMI calculation medical advice?

No. The BMI calculator provides an informational estimate only. Always consult a licensed medical professional for personal clinical diagnostics or nutritional recommendations.
