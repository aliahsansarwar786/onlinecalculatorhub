Frequently Asked Questions

How is Fahrenheit converted to Celsius?

The formula to convert Fahrenheit (°F) to Celsius (°C) is: °C = (°F - 32) * 5 / 9. For example, 32°F converts to exactly 0°C.

How many pounds (lbs) are in one kilogram (kg)?

1 kilogram equals approximately 2.20462 pounds. The exact international avoirdupois pound is legally defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms.

Are temperature conversions proportional like distance?

No. Distance and mass have absolute zero baselines and can be converted via simple constant multipliers. Temperature scales (Celsius and Fahrenheit) have different zero offset benchmarks, necessitating subtraction or addition of 32 degrees.

Which gallon standard is used for volume conversion?

This converter uses the United States liquid gallon standard, where 1 US gallon is equivalent to 3.78541 litres.
