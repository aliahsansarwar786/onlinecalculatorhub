Mathematical EMI Formula

The standard equated monthly installment (reducing balance method) is formulated as:

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)^n] / [(1 + r)^n − 1] Where: P = Principal loan amount r = Monthly interest rate = (Annual Rate / 12) / 100 n = Total number of monthly installments Total Repayment = EMI × n Total Interest = Total Repayment − P

Worked Example

Loan Parameters: Principal P = 1,000,000 PKR, Annual Rate = 12%, Tenure = 60 months (5 years).

Step 1 (Monthly Rate): r = 12 / 1200 = 0.01 (1% per month).

Step 2 (Compounding Factor): (1 + 0.01)^60 = 1.01^60 ≈ 1.816697

Step 3 (Numerator): 1,000,000 × 0.01 × 1.816697 = 18,166.97

Step 4 (Denominator): 1.816697 − 1 = 0.816697

Step 5 (Monthly EMI): 18,166.97 / 0.816697 ≈ 22,244.45 PKR (approx 22,244 PKR/month).

Totals: Total paid = 1,334,667 PKR; Total interest = 334,667 PKR.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified calendar date each month to pay off both principal and interest over the loan tenure.

How does the loan tenure affect total interest paid?

A longer loan tenure lowers your monthly payment (EMI) but significantly increases the cumulative total interest paid over the life of the loan. A shorter tenure increases monthly payments but reduces total interest.

What currency formats are supported?

You can calculate in Pakistani Rupee (PKR), United States Dollar (USD), UAE Dirham (AED), British Pound (GBP), or Indian Rupee (INR) with localized formatting.

Does this calculation include banking processing fees or insurance?

No. This tool computes standard reducing-balance interest and principal repayment. Lender administrative fees, processing charges, insurance, and taxes are not included.
